# 2026-11-15 Check Price iPhone: thread detail (report + chat)

- Author: Claude agent (Opus), for Andrew, one of five agents working in parallel on `checkprice-ios`.
- Instructions: owner spec "each thread stores more detailed information if the user chooses to see it", has saving and hearting, and "the user can choose to chat in these threads"; the report is the main selling point, so it must be clear and beautiful. Task rules: own `Screens/Threads/ThreadDetail*` and `Chat*`, keep `ThreadDetailView(threadID:)`, keep to the prototype report's information, don't change `ThreadStore`'s API.
- Changed: `ThreadDetailView` shows, top to bottom: retailer and when it was checked, with heart and "Save & alert me" (watch price) buttons; the verdict on glass over blue light with the product summary as the reason; today's price against the predicted low; a 12-month price chart; "The signal" (what to do and why); then the chat. Heart and save sit in the content, not the navigation bar, so they show both pushed from Threads and as Home's sheet. The tab bar hides while it is pushed.
- Changed: the chart's history is demo data, made per product from its price and predicted low and seeded by its name so it never changes between opens (`ThreadDetailHistory.swift`). Past sales (Presidents' Day, Prime Day, Labor Day, Black Friday) are shaded; Black Friday reaches the year's low. The report says the history is sample data and that saving sends no alerts yet.
- Changed: chat in Worldscape bubbles (`ChatColumn.swift`): sent messages rise in, hopping dots wait in a reply bubble, the reply writes itself in word by word behind the glow caret, and the view scrolls to the newest line. An empty chat offers three suggested questions as chips. The blue input bar is pinned to the bottom (`ChatInputBar.swift`) and calls `ThreadStore.send`. Replies are still the store's canned text.
- Checks: `xcodegen generate`; the whole app type-checked as Mac Catalyst iOS 18 in Swift 5 and 6 modes with no errors or warnings in these files. The history generator ran in a small harness for all three demo products: lows, usual prices and sale dates came out as intended, with no duplicate days.
